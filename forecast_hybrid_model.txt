Two-stage LSTM + XGBoost hybrid forecaster for Aedes aegypti egg counts, with 4-fold cross-validation

Bidirectional LSTM predicts weekly egg counts from an 8-week sequence of environmental, climatic, and autoregressive features; an XGBoost model then learns to correct the LSTM's residual error using the flattened input window, week number, and the LSTM's own prediction. Final forecast = LSTM output + XGBoost residual correction. Includes a 4-fold cross-validation routine over the real breeding seasons, each fold training on the synthetic series plus the other seasons and evaluating against a fixed held-out test set.
