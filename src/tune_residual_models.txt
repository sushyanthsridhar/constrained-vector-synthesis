Randomized hyperparameter search for XGBoost, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting residual correctors

Tunes all three tree-based models via RandomizedSearchCV (20 sampled configurations each, 4-fold cross-validation on the training data) to correct the LSTM's residual error, using an identical 186-dimensional feature set across all three (flattened 8-week predictor window, week number, LSTM prediction), so results are directly comparable. Training residuals are generated out-of-fold via compute_oof_lstm_predictions (the same inner-fold procedure used in forecast_hybrid_model.py), so no model here is tuned against its own in-sample LSTM error. Selects the best configuration per model by cross-validated R² on the training data only, then reports its performance on the held-out test set. XGBoost is searched over the ranges in the paper's Appendix E (continuous sampling); Random Forest and Gradient Boosting over the discrete grids in Table 12.
