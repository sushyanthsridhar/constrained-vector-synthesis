SARIMAX gap reconstruction and literature-derived trend amplification

Fits a SARIMAX model with NASA POWER temperature/humidity/precipitation as exogenous regressors (order selected by AIC over a widened grid, p,q up to 3 and seasonal P,Q up to 2, seasonal period = 28 weeks) to the spatially-averaged weekly mean egg count pooled across the 127 training traps for 2009-2013, then forecasts the missing 2013-2022 decade (9 breeding seasons of 28 weeks) using the real NASA POWER climate values for those calendar years as the exogenous forecast inputs (climate data exists for that period even though egg-count data does not). A compounding trend-amplification factor derived from a literature-reported 9.5%-per-century Aedes aegypti abundance increase (~0.09%/year) is then applied, clipped to the empirical 90% confidence interval per week. The most recently imputed season (2022, immediately preceding the real 2023-2024 test period) is used as the single 28-week temporal template consumed by synthesize_egg_count_series.py; the empirical week-to-week differences of the real 2009-2013 weekly means are also output for the volatility constraint. An order-sensitivity helper (evaluate_order_sensitivity) supports the paper's +-1 AR/MA robustness check once a final order is fixed via SARIMAX_ORDER_OVERRIDE. Never uses 2023-2024 data at any step.
