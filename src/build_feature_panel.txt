Assembles the 23-raw-feature trap-week panel and the 8-week sequence tensors

Merges per-trap weekly egg counts, NASA POWER climatic variables, and LANDSAT-7 environmental indices into the trap-week panel in Table 2, derives the calendar month from the sampling date, applies the 3-week rolling average to precipitation before lagging, and builds the autoregressive lag features (1, 2, 4, and 52 weeks for egg counts; 2, 4, and 52 for temperature and humidity; 2, 4, 6, and 52 for rainfall) chronologically within each trap. The four 52-week lag features are zero-filled for the 31 traps first deployed in 2023-2024, since those traps have no observation a year before any forecast origin in the test season. build_sequence_tensor then extracts the (8, 23) window ending at a given forecast origin, extending into a trap's pre-season history for origins in the first seven weeks of a season rather than zero-padding.
