Constrained random-walk generator for synthetic Aedes aegypti egg-count series

Generates 2,000 synthetic 28-week ovitrap series (Algorithm 1). draw_seasonal_totals samples each trap's seasonal total from a Gaussian Mixture Model; compute_trap_scales converts that into a per-series scale factor; compute_global_anchor, compute_template_band, and compute_volatility_band precompute the magnitude, template, and volatility bands the walk is clipped to; generate_one_series runs the walk for a single trap, bounded by a SARIMAX-based temporal trend template rescaled to match the sampled total, a volatility constraint on week-to-week change, and a magnitude constraint keeping values within both the GMM intensity anchors and a 90% confidence interval of the real 2009-2013 data; generate_all_series loops it across the batch.
