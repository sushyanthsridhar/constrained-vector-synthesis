Discrete Fourier Transform spectral validation for synthetic egg-count series

Compares the Fourier amplitude spectra of real vs. synthetic weekly egg-count series for each year, computing the amplitude discrepancy (Error_k) at the first few dominant frequencies. Uses one normalization convention throughout, a_k = Re(F_k)/N, b_k = -Im(F_k)/N, A_k = |F_k|/N, matching the manuscript's own equations in Sec. 2.4 exactly, for both the printable Fourier terms and the reported amplitude spectrum and Error_k. Small errors at these low-frequency components confirm the synthetic data preserves the real seasonal breeding cycle; this is the quality-assurance check described in Section 2.4, not an independent validation.
